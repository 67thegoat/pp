About KOM India

KOM India (Kaizen of Machines India), a brand under Purple Panther Private Limited, is committed to delivering world-class earthmoving machine parts for tough jobs.

With over 5 years of trusted manufacturing and trading experience through our Indian brand Dev Industries, KOM India is ready to serve global markets with high-quality Genuine, OEM, and Aftermarket parts.

Headquartered in Delhi, India, our company is proud to operate a robust local supply chain, empowering Indian manufacturing and ensuring fast, efficient production and delivery.

We currently operate in around 256 major towns in these 9 Indian states: Assam, Delhi, Haryana, Himachal Pradesh, Madhya Pradesh, Punjab, Rajasthan, Uttar Pradesh, Uttarakhand.

Our leadership team includes two dynamic women directors: Surabhi Jain, a Mumbai University Engineering topper, and Chandni Sethi, a business-savvy entrepreneur. Together, they bring balance, integrity, and innovation to our operations.
